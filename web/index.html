Atlantis

The Lost Tales · Atlantis II

Seth, the queen's new guard, arrives in Atlantis just as Queen Rhea disappears; two thousand years later, in Tibet, a new hero is called to save the world. Atlantis Native is a new engine for Cryo's adventures of 1997 and 1999: both games whole, faithful to the originals, playable here.

It does not include the games themselves: their pictures, videos, voices and music belong to their publisher, so it plays them from your own copy. Choose the folder of your game:

Atlantis: The Lost Tales

  • GOG.com: the folder where the game is installed.
  • CD: a folder holding atlantis.exe and BIGCD1.BIG to BIGCD4.BIG, copied from the four discs.
  • DVD (Cryo, 1998): the DVD drive, or a copy of a side (the folder holding FACE_A or FACE_B); then choose the language.

Atlantis II

  • GOG.com: the folder where the game is installed (the one holding Game.exe and DATAS_A2).
  • CD (or the CD with patch 1.01): the folder where the game is installed (the one holding atlantis2.exe, wam and scenar), with a copy of each of the four CDs in it, for example in folders CD1 to CD4, each holding the disc's DATAS_A2 folder. The browser reads files, not CD images (.iso…): copy the discs' files.

Your files stay on your computer: the browser only reads them, a part at a time when the game needs it, and the players and saves are kept in this browser. How to get the game files. Best with a mouse and a keyboard; F10 (or O) opens the options, F11 (or F) the full screen.
