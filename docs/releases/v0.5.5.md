Atlantis Native 0.5.5: Atlantis II in the browser.

You need your own copy of the games: Atlantis Native contains no game
data. Play both games in your browser at
https://ripleyxlr8.github.io/atlantis-native/ or
https://ripleyxlr8.itch.io/atlantis-native.

## New in 0.5.5

### Atlantis II in the browser

- **The web page plays Atlantis II**, as the desktop does: the whole
  game, its scripts, dialogs and 3D talking heads, music, videos and
  menus. Choose your game folder on the page; the browser asks to
  confirm that the page may read it. Its files are read inside the
  browser, a part at a time when the game needs it (its 2.4 GB are
  never loaded whole), and sent nowhere.
- **What to pick**:
  - the GOG.com edition: the folder where the game is installed (the one
    holding `Game.exe` and `DATAS_A2`);
  - the CD (version 1.00, or patched to 1.01): the folder of the
    installed game (the one holding `atlantis2.exe`, `wam` and
    `scenar`), with a copy of each of the four CDs in it, for example in
    folders `CD1` to `CD4`, each holding the disc's `DATAS_A2` folder.
    The copies may also sit beside the installed folder: then pick the
    folder holding both. The browser reads files, not CD images
    (`.iso`...): copy the discs' files;
  - a folder holding both games: the page asks which one to play.
- **The same game as on the desktop**: the saves (the original's files,
  kept in the browser), F10's options menu with the HD mode, widescreen
  and the bug-fix box, full screen (F, F11 or the page's button). The
  mouse turns the view once a click has taken it (the browser's pointer
  lock; Escape lets it go); where the lock is refused, clicks act at
  once and the arrow keys turn the view. The sound starts with the first
  click or key, as browsers require. A hidden page (another tab, the
  browser minimised) holds the game, its clock and its sound, as the
  desktop does without the focus.
- **Full speed**: the HD mode's panorama and the dialogs' pictures are
  drawn by the graphics card. Measured in Edge on an i7-9700K, a frame
  takes 1.5 ms (original graphics) and 3.8 ms (HD) while the view turns,
  4.4 ms and 11.1 ms in a dialog with a 3D head, for 16.7 ms available;
  Chrome measures the same.
- **Tested** from the start-up intro to Tibet's first dialog: in Edge
  with GOG.com's French edition, the French CD's installed folder with
  copies of its four CDs, and the same patched to 1.01; in Chrome and
  Firefox with the GOG.com edition.

Its limits:

- Safari has not been tried with Atlantis II.
- A browser does not let a page keep a folder: choose it again at each
  visit (there is no game library). The four CDs are needed at once (no
  disc can be added while the game runs).
- On a computer about half as fast as a recent desktop, the HD mode runs
  a dialog somewhat slower than it should: choose the original graphics
  in F10's options there (they keep the full pace).
- The graphics card computes the HD view in single precision: about one
  pixel in a thousand differs by a level or two from the desktop's.
- A browser gives a site about 5 MB of storage, shared by both games:
  thousands of Atlantis II saves fit (one or two KB each).

### Both games

- **A save the browser refuses** (its storage full) is now shown at the
  top of the page, rather than only logged.
- The page shows what to pick for each game side by side, and its key
  reminders for the game being played.

## Downloads

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 and later, 64-bit | `atlantis-native-windows-x64.zip` |
| macOS 11 and later, Apple Silicon | `atlantis-native-macos-arm64.dmg` |
| macOS 11 and later, Intel | `atlantis-native-macos-x64.dmg` |
| Linux x86-64 (glibc 2.35+, ALSA) | `atlantis-native-linux-x64.tar.gz` |
| Linux 64-bit ARM, e.g. Raspberry Pi 4 or 5 | `atlantis-native-linux-arm64.tar.gz` |
| Web (both games) | play online, or `atlantis-native-web.zip` on the [nightly release](https://github.com/ripleyXLR8/atlantis-native/releases/tag/nightly) |

The builds are not signed: Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time
("More info", then "Run anyway"). On macOS, open the app once, then go to
System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway".

section for what to pick in the browser).
