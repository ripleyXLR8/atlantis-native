Atlantis Native 0.5.11: a new bug fix for Atlantis II, the wooden girl in Ireland's book.

You need your own copy of the games: Atlantis Native contains no game
data. Play both games in your browser at
https://ripleyxlr8.github.io/atlantis-native/ or
https://ripleyxlr8.itch.io/atlantis-native.

## New in 0.5.11

### Atlantis II

- **A new bug fix, on by default: the wooden girl leaves Ireland's book.**
  Once the goddess has spoken of the statue or of the poet, the girl of the
  book is freed, but the original program still draws the wooden girl beside
  her: the script hides her as a character, while in the book she is an
  object. With the fix only the freed girl is there. `--original` and "All
  off" keep the original behaviour, and the fix has its own switch in the
  options, `fix_wooden_girl`. Reported by Klaas Van den Broeck (@Klaasvdb),
  github.com/Klaasvdb/atlantis2-notes.
- Two more of the original's bugs that he reported were checked and get no
  switch, because the game never reaches them: a dialog in which the hero
  speaks last leaves the view turned away (only dialogs the game never plays
  end that way), and a music stopped by the scripts could not be started
  again (no script asks for the same music after stopping it). The port keeps
  the original's behaviour for both; the [technical notes](https://github.com/ripleyXLR8/atlantis-native/blob/main/docs/TECHNICAL.md#enhancements)
  give the details.
