Atlantis Native 0.8.1: a portable mode, and the fixes from a new
comparison of the four games with their original programs.

You need your own copy of the games: Atlantis Native contains no game
data. Play the games in your browser at
https://ripleyxlr8.github.io/atlantis-native/ or
https://ripleyxlr8.itch.io/atlantis-native.

## New in 0.8.1

### Portable mode, `--data-dir` and `--help`

- **Portable mode:** put an empty file named `portable.txt` (or a folder
  named `data`) next to the program (on macOS, next to `Atlantis
  Native.app`) and the saves, settings, game library and log go in that
  `data` folder, made if missing, instead of your user folder. Deleting
  the program's folder then removes the game's files too. Without the
  file or the folder nothing changes: your saves stay where they were.
  To move them, make the `data` folder and copy your user folder's
  contents into it (see the [guide](../GUIDE.md#where-the-files-live)).
- **`--data-dir <folder>`** keeps everything of that run in `<folder>`,
  over every other rule; then come `ATLANTIS_NATIVE_DATA`, the portable
  mode and the per-user folder.
- **`--help`** (or `-h`) prints every option, one line each (the
  development ones apart), and these rules.

### Closer to the originals

Each game was compared again with its original program, and every
difference found was fixed:

- **Atlantis Evolution (GOG.com):** a new bug fix, `fix_skip_films` (on
  by default, off with `--original`): Escape or Space skips a film. The
  view coasts briefly after a drag, as in the original; the screen-edge
  strips and the help button behave as the original's do.
- **Atlantis Evolution (2004 CD, preview):** the music is cut as the
  original cuts it on the start page and when loading, effects stop on
  Escape to the menu, and the volume sliders store the original's values.
- **Atlantis III:** the interdimension's swaying camera; the sounds of
  the place animations; skipping a film or pressing Escape in the warp
  stops the other sounds; after a film, the right music plays again;
  the start page stops the music; a double click on a move zone skips
  the travel video; the scarab's case drawn for the screen's colour
  depth; the inventory bar's scrolling and clicks; the films' volume.
- **Atlantis II:** music changes and the CD page's music; the blinking
  buttons and their tooltips; the load page's erase button shown only
  with a save; a dialog's first line; the close-up viewer drawn at
  800 x 600; clicks during a music change.
- **Atlantis:** small fixes in the options menu, the bow, the puzzles'
  pointer after loading and the order of saved games.
- **Game library:** an edition with a year in its name is shown like the
  others.
