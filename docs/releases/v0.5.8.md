Atlantis Native 0.5.8: Atlantis II from GOG.com in four languages, and the Czech CD.

You need your own copy of the games: Atlantis Native contains no game
data. Play both games in your browser at
https://ripleyxlr8.github.io/atlantis-native/ or
https://ripleyxlr8.itch.io/atlantis-native.

## New in 0.5.8

### Atlantis II

- **GOG.com's English, French, German and Spanish installations are
  supported.** GOG.com installs the same program for all four languages;
  only the texts and voices differ. Atlantis Native tells the language
  from the installed texts and shows it in the edition's name, for
  example *Atlantis II, GOG.com, German (patch 1.01)*. Every subtitle is
  laid out as the original lays it out, every letter is drawn (French
  `œ`, German `ß`, Spanish `ñ ¿ ¡`) and every voice the installation
  holds plays.
- **Films 19, 55 and 66 play GOG's English soundtrack in every
  language**, as the original does: GOG.com placed the English tracks
  in the folder the program reads these three films' sound from. Each
  language's own track is installed but never read.
- **Older GOG.com installations are accepted**: the program of GOG's
  2018 build and of its June 2022 build, shown as *(patch 1.01, 2018
  build)* and *(patch 1.01, June 2022 build)*. They differ from the
  current program only in Windows-specific fixes that Atlantis Native
  does not need, so all three play the same.

### Atlantis: The Lost Tales

- **The Czech CD is supported**: Bohemia Interactive's four-CD release
  (2000), also re-issued with the magazine GAME4U Speciál 2 (2001).
  Choose the folder with `atlantis.exe` and `BIGCD1.BIG` …
  `BIGCD4.BIG`, or the folder of the four disc images. The game shows as
  *CD, Czech (Bohemia Interactive, 1999 build)*; its saves are its own,
  apart from those of a CD with Cryo's patch 1.01.
- **It plays as it shipped.** Its program is Cryo's 1.01 patch, over
  the US CD's data with Czech subtitles, menus, chapter names and
  credits, and English voices. The subtitles are read as a Czech Windows
  shows them (`č`, `ř`, `ů` and the other Czech letters), the menus draw
  the Czech letters of its own fonts, and a player name can be typed
  with `Š Ť Ž š ť ž`. The player screen keeps the program's French
  *NOUVEAU JOUEUR* line, as in the original.
- GOG.com's 2015 installation (its build 3341) was checked: its program
  is Cryo's patch 1.01 with four of GOG's fixes, and Atlantis Native
  plays it as the CD with patch 1.01, as before.

|---|---|

| macOS 11 and later, Apple Silicon | `atlantis-native-macos-arm64.dmg` |

The builds are not signed: Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time
("More info", then "Run anyway"). On macOS, open the app once, then go to
System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway".
