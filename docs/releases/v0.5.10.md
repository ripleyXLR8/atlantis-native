Atlantis Native 0.5.10: Atlantis II in Italian, and three films in the game's own language.

You need your own copy of the games: Atlantis Native contains no game
data. Play both games in your browser at
https://ripleyxlr8.github.io/atlantis-native/ or
https://ripleyxlr8.itch.io/atlantis-native.

## New in 0.5.10

### Atlantis II

- **The Italian *Atlantis Collection* DVD (2014) is supported.** Install the
  game from the DVD and point Atlantis Native at the folder the installer
  creates. It shows as *Atlantis II, Italian Collection (patch 1.01)*, with
  Italian subtitles, menus and voices.
- **A new bug fix, on by default: films 19, 55 and 66 play in the game's own
  language** on GOG.com (French, German, Spanish) and on the Italian
  collection. Their own tracks are installed, but the original program never
  reads them: on GOG.com these three films play in English in every language,
  and on the Italian collection in what sounds like Russian. Nothing changes on
  the CDs or on GOG.com in English. `--original` and "All off" keep the original
  behaviour, and the fix has its own switch in the options, `fix_film_language`.

### Atlantis: The Lost Tales

- **The first game of the same DVD is recognised**: point Atlantis Native at the
  folder its installer creates. It plays as the CD with Cryo's patch 1.01, and
  the game shows it under that name.

The builds are not signed: Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time
("More info", then "Run anyway"). On macOS, open the app once, then go to
System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway".
