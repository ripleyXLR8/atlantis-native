Atlantis Native 0.2.0: every retail edition of the game we could find,
a game library, and playing straight from your CD images.

You need your own copy of the game: Atlantis Native contains no game
data.

## Supported editions

| Release | Languages |
|---|---|
| CD, 2001 build | English |
| CD, 1997 builds | English (May), French (May and August), German, Dutch, Swedish, Spanish, Italian |
| CD with Cryo's patch 1.01 | over the English, French, German, Dutch, Swedish, Spanish or Italian CD |
| GOG.com | English, French, German, Spanish, Italian |

Each edition plays with its own rules, as its original executable does
(reverse engineered and checked against it), and keeps its own saves.

## New in 0.2.0

- **Finding your game**: on the first start Atlantis Native looks for the
  game on your computer (GOG.com and the usual folders) and offers it; it
  explains why it needs the game's files and, when a folder is refused,
  says exactly what is missing (a disc, atlantis.exe, an installer not
  run yet...).
- **CD images**: play straight from images of the four CDs (.iso, .bin/.cue,
  .img, .mdf, .nrg), without extracting anything; or let Atlantis Native
  copy the game from your discs, one after the other.
- **Game library**: several editions, chosen at start-up (or hold Shift),
  or switched from the F10 menu ("Game library...").
- **New editions**: the 1997 builds of the French, German, Dutch, Swedish,
  Spanish and Italian CDs, the English and French May 1997 builds, and
  Cryo's patch 1.01 (see the table).
- **Unknown edition?** One click opens a pre-filled report on GitHub with
  the files' identifiers (no personal data).
- **Widescreen**: no black flash at the start and end of a move.
- **Fixes of the original's bugs** (on by default, F10 to turn off): among
  others the German "ß" of the chapter list drawn as "ss", the Italian
  menus' click zones covering the whole word.
- **Our own icon** for the program file and the macOS app; the macOS Dock
  shows your game's icon while you play.
- A support screen now and then (at most once a week, never in your first
  hours, can be turned off for good), and "Buy me a coffee..." in F10.

## Downloads

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 and later, 64-bit | `atlantis-native-windows-x64.zip` |
| macOS 11 and later, Apple Silicon | `atlantis-native-macos-arm64.dmg` |
| Linux x86-64 (glibc 2.35+, ALSA) | `atlantis-native-linux-x64.tar.gz` |

The web build is on the
[nightly release](https://github.com/ripleyXLR8/atlantis-native/releases/tag/nightly).

The builds are not signed: Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time
("More info", then "Run anyway"). On macOS, open the app once, then go to
System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway".

See the [README](https://github.com/ripleyXLR8/atlantis-native#readme)
and the [player's guide](https://github.com/ripleyXLR8/atlantis-native/blob/main/docs/GUIDE.md).
