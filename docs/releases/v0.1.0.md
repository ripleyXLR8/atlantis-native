The first numbered release of Atlantis Native, a native port of
*Atlantis: The Lost Tales* (Cryo Interactive, 1997), written in Rust.

**The whole game is playable**, from the Cryo logo to the end credits:
panoramas, cinematics, dialogs with 3D talking heads in time with the
voices, the inventory, the characters who follow you, the menus,
checkpoints, game overs and all seventeen puzzles.

You need your own copy of the game: Atlantis Native contains no game
data.

## Supported releases

| Release | Languages |
|---|---|
| English CD, 2001 build | English |
| English retail CD, 1997 build | English (its own 8x8 subtitle font) |
| GOG.com | English, French, German, Spanish, Italian |

The builds are not signed yet: Windows SmartScreen may warn the first
time ("More info", then "Run anyway"); on macOS, right-click the app and
choose Open.

## Highlights

- **Faithful**: the scripts, dialogs, music, 3D heads and puzzles run the
  original's own logic; the heads are drawn with the original's
  arithmetic, pixel for pixel.
- **Options menu (F10)**: original or HD graphics, HD picture options,
  widescreen, and the enhancements, changed while you play.
- **HD mode**: the panoramas and talking heads rendered at the window's
  resolution, sharp subtitles, interface art upscaled with xBR.
- **Widescreen**: the panorama fills a wide window; beside the videos,
  black bars, blurred edges, a zoom, or (for the moves) the panoramas of
  the places left and reached.
- **Fixes of the original's bugs** (on by default, one switch turns them
  off): seamless music loops, talking-head overlays and mouths drawn
  correctly, the snakes puzzle's first-run win, and more.
- **Saves**: the original's own checkpoint files; an existing
  installation's players and checkpoints are taken over.
