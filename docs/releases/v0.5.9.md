Atlantis Native 0.5.9: the North American *Beyond Atlantis* CD, the Brazilian CD of the first game, and a package for handhelds.

You need your own copy of the games: Atlantis Native contains no game
data. Play both games in your browser at
https://ripleyxlr8.github.io/atlantis-native/ or
https://ripleyxlr8.itch.io/atlantis-native.

## New in 0.5.9

### Atlantis II

- **The North American CD is supported**: *Beyond Atlantis* (DreamCatcher,
  2000, 4 CDs). It needs the same files as the French CD: the folder of
  the installed game with the images of its four CDs (or copies of the
  CDs); in the browser, copies of the CDs. It shows as *Atlantis II, CD,
  English (DreamCatcher, 2000)*. It has a program of its own, with version
  1.01's code, and opens on its own DreamCatcher logo.
- **A new bug fix, on by default, keeps subtitles over films visible at
  800 x 600 on every edition.** The DreamCatcher program shows them; the
  French CD, the 1.01 patch and GOG.com's program draw them where the next
  frame covers them. `--original` hides them on those editions, as they
  shipped.

### Atlantis: The Lost Tales

- **The Brazilian Portuguese CD is supported**: MSD Multimídia's *Atlantis:
  A Lenda Perdida* (1997, 4 CDs, dubbed). Choose the folder with
  `atlantis.exe` and `BIGCD1.BIG` … `BIGCD4.BIG`, or the four disc images.
  It shows as *CD, Brazilian Portuguese (MSD, December 1997 build)* and
  plays with its own program: Portuguese voices and subtitles, and menus
  drawn with its own fonts and widths.
- **The only public dump known of that CD has unreadable sectors.** Played
  from it, a few films of disc 2 stop early and one panorama does not show;
  nothing crashes. This comes from the dump: no original disc has been
  checked yet.
- **Deleting a player on the 1997 programs** now writes `players.atl` as the
  original does. Nothing visible changes.

### Handhelds

- **A PortMaster package**, `atlantis-native-portmaster.zip`, for 64-bit ARM
  handhelds, with the first game for now. The gamepad is the mouse, and an
  on-screen keyboard (a grid of letters and digits) types the players'
  names. Its gamepad and keyboard code started from
  [Klaas Van den Broeck's R36S port](https://github.com/Klaasvdb/atlantis-native-handheld).
  Unzip it into the `ports` folder and copy your game's files into
  `ports/atlantisnative/gamedata/`, or drop the zip into PortMaster's
  `autoinstall` folder. It has been played on a Raspberry Pi 5 under
  Batocera with an Xbox pad; it has not been run on an R36S yet.
- **Xbox-style pads that SDL does not recognise**, such as an Xbox Wireless
  Controller over Bluetooth, work without a `controls.ini`.

### Windows

- **A build for Windows on ARM**, `atlantis-native-windows-arm64.zip`.

|---|---|

The builds are not signed: Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time
("More info", then "Run anyway"). On macOS, open the app once, then go to
System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway".
