Atlantis Native 0.5.0: Atlantis II, from its start to its end, and more
editions of the first game.

You need your own copy of the games: Atlantis Native contains no game
data. Play the first game in your browser at
https://ripleyxlr8.github.io/atlantis-native/ or
https://ripleyxlr8.itch.io/atlantis-native.

## New in 0.5.0

### Atlantis II (Beyond Atlantis, 1999)

- **The whole game plays**, on Windows, macOS and Linux: Tibet and its
  mandalas, Ireland, China, Yucatán with Xibalba, the space travel,
  Shamballa, Atlantis and the end credits, with the scripts, dialogs,
  3D talking heads and their lip-sync, music, place sounds, videos, the
  bag bar and the menus run as the original's code does. A test plays
  the whole game with the mouse, from the start-up intro to the credits.
  (The web version does not run Atlantis II yet.)
- **Its puzzles**: Tibet's mandalas; Ireland's Brigid's cross, skull,
  ogham stones and blackbird songs; China's figurines, chest, incense,
  five wheels, pearl and dragon, turtle ring and the labyrinth's souls;
  Yucatán's counting grids; Xibalba's star map, canoe and spider's web;
  Shamballa's fireflies and the octopus fight of Atlantis.
- **The editions**: the French CD (the folder of the installed game,
  with the images of its four CDs or copies of the CDs),
  a CD installation with the official patch 1.01 (one executable for the
  French and the English releases, played as 1.01 plays), and the
  GOG.com edition (1.01 with GOG's own changes; tested with its French
  version). Another executable is refused with its identifiers.
- **HD mode** (optional): the panoramas and the dialogs' 3D heads drawn
  at the window's resolution with the original's projection, sharp
  subtitles and menu texts, and **Widescreen** with black or blurred
  bars beside the videos. With the original graphics, the original's
  640 x 480 and 800 x 600 screens.
- **Saves**: the original's own files, 99 slots per player, from the
  game's menu.
- **Bug fixes** (optional), each with its own switch in F10's
  "Choose the bug fixes...": the music tracks heard to their end, an
  ended track started again, the rare missing music mark (1 in 32768)
  that stops the original with a fatal error, and Ireland's cross and
  ogham clicks. "All off" plays the game as it shipped.

### Atlantis: The Lost Tales

- **Cryo's 1998 DVD-ROM**, in its eight languages: English, French,
  German, Dutch (side A); Spanish, Italian, Swedish and Japanese (side B),
  each language its own edition, from the drive, a copy of a side or the
  images of the sides. The Japanese version draws its subtitles with MS
  PGothic when you have `msgothic.ttc`, else with the bundled Noto Sans
  JP, and its SUBTITLES option switches between Japanese, English and
  none.
- **The Perfect Edition** (Tgames' 2018 package of the DVD's English
  version).
- **The German CD's first pressing** (May 1997 build), as a folder or
  as the four disc images. Thanks to derRichter for the
  [report on GitHub](https://github.com/ripleyXLR8/atlantis-native/issues/6).
- **One switch per bug fix**: F10's "Fix original bugs" shows On, Off
  or Custom, and "Choose the bug fixes..." lists the fixes of the
  edition played, each with what the original does without it. New
  fixes: the DVD's Dutch and Swedish subtitles shown with their accented
  letters, and the Japanese DVD's two "file not found" boxes at start-up
  skipped.
- **The game library knows both games**: it finds Atlantis II's GOG.com
  installation, takes the folder of a CD installation with "Add a game
  folder...", and opens either game. `--list-games` prints the games
  found on the computer.

### Everywhere

- **macOS on Intel**: `atlantis-native-macos-x64.dmg`, beside the Apple
  Silicon build.

## Fixes

- The error box ends on OK only, as the original's: Escape leaves it
  open.
- The credits' fonts are read from the disc in the drive at each credits
  run, as the original does (the German May 1997 CD's discs do not all
  hold the same ones).

## Downloads

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 and later, 64-bit | `atlantis-native-windows-x64.zip` |
| macOS 11 and later, Apple Silicon | `atlantis-native-macos-arm64.dmg` |

The builds are not signed: Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time
("More info", then "Run anyway"). On macOS, open the app once, then go to
System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway".
