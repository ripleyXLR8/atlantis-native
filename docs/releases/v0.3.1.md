Atlantis Native 0.3.1: smoother widescreen, and now on itch.io.

You need your own copy of the game: Atlantis Native contains no game
data. Play in your browser at https://ripleyxlr8.github.io/atlantis-native/
or https://ripleyxlr8.itch.io/atlantis-native.

## New in 0.3.1

- **Widescreen moves**: in the Zoom mode a move's video now zooms in
  from the panorama and back out to the destination's, the whole picture
  as one (the sides rendered at the video's own angles, so that they
  continue it); no more jump when the video ends.
- **Widescreen dialogs**: a character cut by the screen's edge in the
  original is moved to the edge of the wide picture, with its 3D head,
  instead of showing the cut in the middle of the screen.
- **Very wide screens**: a video's sides stop where the panorama's do
  (2.4:1), black beyond.
- **Web version**: the start page in the manner of the game's own start
  screen, on a blue parchment, with the version; the game framed on the
  same parchment, filling the page (and wide with widescreen); the
  graphics card now also composes the HD picture (the browser's work per
  frame about halved again); no black flash before a move's video.
- **itch.io**: the releases are now also published on
  https://ripleyxlr8.itch.io/atlantis-native.

## Downloads

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 and later, 64-bit | `atlantis-native-windows-x64.zip` |
| macOS 11 and later, Apple Silicon | `atlantis-native-macos-arm64.dmg` |
| Linux x86-64 (glibc 2.35+, ALSA) | `atlantis-native-linux-x64.tar.gz` |
| Web | play online, or `atlantis-native-web.zip` on the [nightly release](https://github.com/ripleyXLR8/atlantis-native/releases/tag/nightly) |

The builds are not signed: Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time
("More info", then "Run anyway"). On macOS, open the app once, then go to
System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway".

See the [README](https://github.com/ripleyXLR8/atlantis-native#readme)
and the [player's guide](https://github.com/ripleyXLR8/atlantis-native/blob/main/docs/GUIDE.md).
