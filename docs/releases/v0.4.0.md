Atlantis Native 0.4.0: the Chinese CDs, a Raspberry Pi build, and a
second review against the original.

You need your own copy of the game: Atlantis Native contains no game
data. Play in your browser at https://ripleyxlr8.github.io/atlantis-native/
or https://ripleyxlr8.itch.io/atlantis-native.

## New in 0.4.0

- **The two Chinese CDs**: the Traditional Chinese edition (1998) and the
  Simplified Chinese edition (1999), four discs each, as a folder or as
  disc images. Subtitles are broken into lines and drawn as those CDs do;
  their menus, LOAD list and opening captions use the pictures shipped on
  the discs. Put Windows' `mingliu.ttc` (Traditional) or `simsun.ttc`
  (Simplified) in the game folder to use the font the original used;
  otherwise the bundled Noto Serif TC or SC is used (SIL Open Font License
  1.1). The web version downloads these fonts only for the Chinese CDs.
- **Chinese READMEs**: [繁體中文](https://github.com/ripleyXLR8/atlantis-native/blob/main/README.zh-TW.md)
  and [简体中文](https://github.com/ripleyXLR8/atlantis-native/blob/main/README.zh-CN.md).
- **Linux 64-bit ARM** (Raspberry Pi 4 and 5): `atlantis-native-linux-arm64.tar.gz`.
- **`--fullscreen`** opens the window in full screen, for launchers such as
  Batocera's Ports.
- **O** opens and closes the options menu, as F10 does, for keyboards
  without function keys.
- **Zoomed video sides** (widescreen HD): the zoom is 4 to 5 times faster
  with the same pixels, it takes the first and last 15% of a move's video,
  and the joins between the video and its sides are blurred.
- **A second review against the original game's code**: the inventory
  bar's slides and pick-up, the LOAD picker and ARE YOU SURE, the player
  screen, the dialog line rules, the head renderer and lip-sync, the
  music fades and ambient pans, the warp loop's turn and transition video
  order, several puzzles (rats, snakes and hens, turning pieces), the
  script's subject gates and header angles now follow the original more
  closely.

## Fixes

- No more freeze on the last frame of a move's video before the view.
- A skipped move video no longer leaves its sides on screen.
- The GOG build's players, checkpoints and settings keep their place
  after an update.
- Loading a checkpoint keeps the follower-talk state, so the next
  conversation hides the other followers again.

## Downloads

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 and later, 64-bit | `atlantis-native-windows-x64.zip` |
| macOS 11 and later, Apple Silicon | `atlantis-native-macos-arm64.dmg` |
| Linux x86-64 (glibc 2.35+, ALSA) | `atlantis-native-linux-x64.tar.gz` |
| Linux 64-bit ARM, e.g. Raspberry Pi 4 or 5 | `atlantis-native-linux-arm64.tar.gz` |
| Web | play online, or `atlantis-native-web.zip` on the [nightly release](https://github.com/ripleyXLR8/atlantis-native/releases/tag/nightly) |

The builds are not signed: Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time
("More info", then "Run anyway"). On macOS, open the app once, then go to
System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway".
