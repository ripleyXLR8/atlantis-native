Atlantis Native 0.5.7: the US retail CD.

You need your own copy of the games: Atlantis Native contains no game
data. Play both games in your browser at
https://ripleyxlr8.github.io/atlantis-native/ or
https://ripleyxlr8.itch.io/atlantis-native.

## New in 0.5.7

### Atlantis: The Lost Tales

- **The US retail CD is supported**: the four-CD release Interplay
  distributed in the United States in 1997, whose program is an August
  1997 build of its own. Choose the folder with `atlantis.exe` and
  `BIGCD1.BIG` … `BIGCD4.BIG`, or the folder of the four disc images. The
  game shows as *CD, English (US, Interplay, August 1997 build)*; its
  saves are its own, apart from those of the May 1997 English CD.
- **It plays as it shipped.** Its program is the August 1997 French CD's
  with English texts, so it behaves as the August 1997 French and German
  CDs do, not as the May 1997 English CD: subtitles drawn in Arial
  (Liberation Sans, as for the 2001 build), typed player names kept as
  typed, a second death in the same section followed by the next
  narrator line. Its only code changes are two rules of the 2001 build,
  which Atlantis Native applies to it and to no other 1997 CD: the
  navigate cursor, not the talk cursor, over the characters 0x219 and
  0x21a, and Rhea always on camera 2 in the dialogs.
- **Its data are the August 1997 revision in English**: the May 1997
  English CD's dialog texts with 31 lines corrected or in American
  spelling, and a few script changes of that revision (chapters 15, 47
  and 70). F10's bug fixes for it are the August 1997 French CD's.

|---|---|
| Windows 10 and later, 64-bit | `atlantis-native-windows-x64.zip` |
| macOS 11 and later, Apple Silicon | `atlantis-native-macos-arm64.dmg` |

The builds are not signed: Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time
("More info", then "Run anyway"). On macOS, open the app once, then go to
System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway".
