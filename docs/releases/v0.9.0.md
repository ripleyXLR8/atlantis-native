Atlantis Native 0.9.0: the fifth game, *The Secrets of Atlantis: The
Sacred Legacy* (2006), from its French DVD, on the desktop and in the
browser.

You need your own copy of the games: Atlantis Native contains no game
data. Play the games in your browser at
https://ripleyxlr8.github.io/atlantis-native/ or
https://ripleyxlr8.itch.io/atlantis-native.

## New in 0.9.0

### The Secrets of Atlantis (Atlantis V)

- **The French DVD** (*L'Héritage sacré*, Nobilis, 2006) plays from start
  to end: the nine chapters, the twenty puzzles, poker, the dialogs and
  their subject menu, the inventory and the examine view, the saves, the
  deaths and the credits. The game's own Lua 5.0 scripts run as they
  are, in a Lua that lays its tables out as Lua 5.0.3 does.
- **Any form of your copy:** the DVD in its drive, its image (`.iso`), a
  copy of its files, or the installed game. Add it to the game library
  like the other games.
- **Copy to this computer:** a game read from the DVD or its image can be
  copied once to your data folder (about 1.4 GB for the French DVD) from
  the game library (the link on its folder line, or the C key), so that
  it then plays from the hard disk. `--atlantis5-install <disc or image>`
  does the same from the command line.
- **In the browser:** pick the DVD's image, a copy of its files or the
  installed game; the saves stay in the browser's storage. Each start
  reads about 450 MB of the image before the logos.
- As in the original: the game's cursors in play and in the menus, the
  logos' sound, the music lowered during dialogs and films, the films'
  pace, the transitions' fade, the system's Lucida fonts on Windows
  (Liberation Sans elsewhere), and the 3D characters' lip-sync.
- Not yet: the international DVD (English, German, Italian, Spanish) is
  read the same way but has not been tried; Android does not play this
  game yet. See the [guide](../GUIDE.md#the-secrets-of-atlantis).

- **Atlantis Evolution:** its script bindings' table writes take Lua's
  collector barrier, as `lua_rawset` does.
- The commit shown next to the version is the build's own again.

## Downloads

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 and later, 64-bit | `atlantis-native-windows-x64.zip` |
| Windows 11 on ARM | `atlantis-native-windows-arm64.zip` |
| macOS 11 and later, Apple Silicon | `atlantis-native-macos-arm64.dmg` |
| macOS 11 and later, Intel | `atlantis-native-macos-x64.dmg` |
| Linux x86-64 (glibc 2.35+, ALSA) | `atlantis-native-linux-x64.tar.gz` |
| Linux 64-bit ARM, e.g. Raspberry Pi 4 or 5 | `atlantis-native-linux-arm64.tar.gz` |

| Web (the five games) | play online, or `atlantis-native-web.zip` on the [nightly release](https://github.com/ripleyXLR8/atlantis-native/releases/tag/nightly) |

The builds are not signed: Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time
("More info", then "Run anyway"). On macOS, open the app once, then go to
System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway".

See the [README](https://github.com/ripleyXLR8/atlantis-native#readme)
(its table of supported editions) and the
