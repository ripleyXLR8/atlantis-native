Copy the files of your own Atlantis: The Lost Tales here (a CD release or
GOG.com): the folder holding atlantis.exe, BIGCD1.BIG ... BIGCD4.BIG and its
subfolders (WAV, WAM, SCENAR...).

Only Atlantis: The Lost Tales plays here: the files of Atlantis II or
Atlantis III are not supported by this port.
